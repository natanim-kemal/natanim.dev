I build systems, and this is where I write about what I learn while building them.

The blog will stay close to the work I actually do:

- **Backend work**: APIs, data models, PostgreSQL, and the server side of products people use without thinking about it.
- **Security**: ModSecurity, OWASP CRS, Coraza, WAF audit tooling, and what the logs taught me.
- **Products**: shipping Flutter and Next.js apps from first sketch to something people open every day.

I care about the parts of engineering that happen between the demo and the incident: the schema that was fine until it wasn't, the rule that blocked the wrong request, the deploy that worked because nobody noticed it.

Expect short, practical notes with code and configuration you can actually use. No threads, no hot takes, just what I figured out, and how.
